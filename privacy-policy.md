Privacy Policy — Dreamy Pin Scheduler

Last updated: 11 November 2026

1. About this application

Dreamy Pin Scheduler is a private desktop application developed for Dreamy Digital Visions, an independent art business selling digital and physical wall art through Etsy.

The application is intended solely to manage the business owner's Pinterest account. It is not offered as a public service.

2. Information accessed

With the account owner's authorisation, the application may access:

Pinterest account identification and connected boards.

Pins and associated information.

Pin analytics, including impressions, saves and outbound clicks.

Authorised Pinterest access tokens needed to operate the application.

3. How information is used

Information is used exclusively to:

Create, organise and schedule Pinterest Pins.

Publish approved content to selected Pinterest boards.

Manage Pin titles, descriptions, hashtags, alt text and destination links.

Retrieve Pinterest performance statistics.

Monitor scheduled publications and publishing errors.

4. Data storage and security

The application is designed to operate locally on the owner's Windows computer.

Pin images, scheduling information, publishing records and authentication credentials will be stored locally using appropriate security measures.

The application does not require a separately hosted cloud database.

5. Sharing information

The application communicates with Pinterest through its official API for authorised publishing and analytics functions.

Personal information is not sold, rented or shared with advertisers or unrelated third parties by the application.

Pinterest processes information according to its own privacy policy.

6. Data retention and deletion

Locally stored application data may be deleted by the owner. Pinterest access may be revoked through the account's authorised app settings.

Content already published on Pinterest remains subject to Pinterest's own data retention and deletion policies.

7. Children's privacy

The application is not intended for children and does not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the application's functionality or data handling practices change.
